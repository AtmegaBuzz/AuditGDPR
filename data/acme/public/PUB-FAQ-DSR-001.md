*Last reviewed: 12 May 2026. Questions about this page or about your rights? Contact us at privacy@acme.example, or contact our Data Protection Officer, Tomás Reyes, at the same address.*

## 1. About these FAQs

These FAQs explain the data protection rights available to Acme's customers and to the end users whose data our customers process through our platform. They cover your rights of access, portability and erasure, how to exercise them, and the deadlines you can expect from us.

Acme acts in two ways. For its own customers' account data, website visitors and marketing, Acme is a controller — meaning we decide how and why that data is used. For the end-user data our customers store in our platform, Acme acts as a processor, handling that data only on our customers' documented instructions.

If you have a question about your rights, contact our Data Protection Officer, Tomás Reyes, at privacy@acme.example. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including the Irish Data Protection Commission, Acme's lead supervisory authority.

## 2. How do I submit a request about my personal data?

There are two ways to make a request:

1. **By email.** Send your request to privacy@acme.example. You can ask for access to your personal data, a copy of it, portability of it, or erasure of it. Our Data Protection Officer, Tomás Reyes, is the contact point for these requests.
2. **Self-service.** If you are an Acme Beacon account holder, you can export or delete your own account data at any time through the **Beacon Settings > Privacy** page, without needing to contact us.

Whichever route you choose, we respond without undue delay and in any event within one month of receiving your request.

**Identity verification.** To protect your data, we may need to confirm that the person making a request is really the person the data is about. Where we have reasonable doubts about your identity, we may ask you for additional information to confirm it, as permitted by Article 12(6) GDPR. We only ask for what is necessary to verify you.

If you are an end user of one of our customers (rather than an Acme account holder), please contact the customer organisation directly, as they are the controller of your data — though you may also contact us at privacy@acme.example and we will direct your request to the right place.

## 3. How quickly will Acme respond?

We respond to requests about your personal data without undue delay and in any event within one month of receiving your request, as required by Article 12(3) GDPR. This one-month deadline applies to requests for access, portability and erasure alike, whether you submit them by email to privacy@acme.example or (for account data) through the Beacon Settings > Privacy page.

**Extensions.** If your request is complex or you submit a number of requests, Article 12(3) GDPR allows us to extend the response period by up to two further months. If we do, we will tell you within one month of receiving your request that an extension is being used and explain why — for example, because the request covers data held across multiple systems or requires careful review of third-party interests.

**If we do not act.** If we do not respond to your request within these timeframes, or we refuse it in whole or in part, we will inform you within one month of the reasons, and of your right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — including the Irish Data Protection Commission — and to seek a judicial remedy, in line with Article 12(4) GDPR.

## 4. Accessing your data (right of access)

Under Article 15 GDPR, you have the right to obtain confirmation from Acme that we hold personal data about you, a copy of that data, and supplementary information about how we process it — including the purposes, the categories of recipients, the retention periods and your rights.

To request a copy of your personal data, contact us at privacy@acme.example. Our Data Protection Officer, Tomás Reyes, is the contact point for these requests. If you are an Acme Beacon account holder, you can also export your account data yourself at any time through the Beacon Settings > Privacy page (see section 5 on portability).

**Cost.** The first copy of your personal data is provided free of charge, as required by Article 15(3) GDPR. We will only charge a reasonable fee, or refuse a request, where it is manifestly unfounded or excessive — for example, where the same request is repeated without new circumstances — in line with Article 12(5) GDPR.

**Protecting others.** When we provide a copy of your data, Article 15(4) GDPR requires that we do not adversely affect the rights and freedoms of others. This means that some information in your copy may be redacted where it contains other people's personal data.

**Verifying your identity.** Where we have reasonable doubts about your identity, we may ask for additional information to confirm it before providing your data, as permitted by Article 12(6) GDPR.

We respond to access requests without undue delay and in any event within one month of receipt, extendable by two further months for complex requests as permitted by Article 12(3) GDPR.

## 5. Exporting and moving your data (data portability)

Under Article 20 GDPR, you have the right to receive the personal data you have provided to Acme in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and to transmit that data to another organisation where the processing is based on your consent or on a contract and is carried out by automated means.

**Self-service export.** If you are an Acme Beacon account holder, you can export your account data at any time through the **Beacon Settings > Privacy** page. Exports are provided in JSON and CSV formats — both structured, commonly used and machine-readable — so you can store the data or move it to another provider.

**By email.** You can also make a portability request by contacting us at privacy@acme.example, where our Data Protection Officer, Tomás Reyes, is the contact point.

We respond to portability requests without undue delay and in any event within one month of receipt, extendable by two further months for complex requests as permitted by Article 12(3) GDPR. As with access requests, where we have reasonable doubts about your identity we may ask for additional information to confirm it under Article 12(6) GDPR, and any data provided will not adversely affect the rights and freedoms of others.

## 6. Deleting your data (right to erasure)

Under Article 17 GDPR, you have the right to ask Acme to erase your personal data without undue delay in circumstances such as where the data is no longer necessary for the purposes we collected it for, where you withdraw consent and no other legal ground applies, or where the data is being processed unlawfully.

**How to request deletion.** There are two ways:

1. **By email.** Send your request to privacy@acme.example, where our Data Protection Officer, Tomás Reyes, is the contact point.
2. **Self-service.** If you are an Acme Beacon account holder, you can delete your own account data at any time through the **Beacon Settings > Privacy** page, without needing to contact us.

**How quickly we respond.** We respond to erasure requests within one month of receiving your request, as required by Article 12(3) GDPR. If your request is complex or you submit a number of requests, we may extend this period by up to two further months, and we will tell you within one month that we are doing so and why. Where we have reasonable doubts about your identity, we may ask for additional information to confirm it under Article 12(6) GDPR before deleting anything.

**When erasure may be refused.** We may refuse an erasure request in whole or in part where an exception under Article 17(3) GDPR applies — for example, where we must keep certain data to comply with a legal obligation, or where the data is needed for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. If we refuse your request in whole or in part, we will explain why within one month.

**Notifying others.** Where your personal data has been disclosed to other recipients and is erased at your request, we notify each recipient of the erasure, unless doing so is impossible or would involve disproportionate effort, in line with Article 19 GDPR.

## 7. What happens after your data is deleted?

Once your personal data has been erased at your request, Acme takes an extra step required by Article 19 GDPR: where your data has been disclosed to other recipients, we notify each of those recipients that the data has been erased, so that they can take action on their own records. We do this unless doing so is impossible or would involve disproportionate effort.

**Your right to know who was told.** On request, we will also tell you which recipients we notified of the erasure.

If you have questions about the recipients notified in connection with your erasure request, contact our Data Protection Officer, Tomás Reyes, at privacy@acme.example. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including the Irish Data Protection Commission.

## 8. Requests about end users of Acme's customers

If you are an end user of one of Acme's customers — for example, someone whose contact details or activity data a customer organisation stores in our platform — the customer organisation, not Acme, decides how and why your data is used. The customer is the controller of your personal data, and Acme processes that data on its behalf as a processor.

**Who to contact.** Please direct requests about your personal data to the customer organisation you interact with. They are responsible for responding to your rights of access, portability and erasure within the deadlines set out in the GDPR.

**How Acme helps.** Acme does not sit on the sidelines. Under our data processing agreement with each customer, and as required by Article 28(3)(e) GDPR, we assist our customers in responding to your access and portability requests — for example, by helping them locate and provide the data about you that is stored in our platform.

If you are unsure who to contact, you can email privacy@acme.example and we will point you to the right organisation. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including the Irish Data Protection Commission.

## 9. Complaints and further help

If you have a question about your rights, or you are unhappy with how we have handled your request, you can contact Acme's Data Protection Officer, Tomás Reyes, at privacy@acme.example. He is Acme's contact point for all requests about your personal data.

**Your right to complain.** You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority under Article 77 GDPR. In Ireland, this is the Irish Data Protection Commission, which is Acme's lead supervisory authority. If you live in another EU member state, you can also complain to your local supervisory authority. You also have the right to seek a judicial remedy.

**Further detail.** For more information about how Acme uses personal data, your rights and how to exercise them, please read the Acme Privacy Notice, available on our website.
