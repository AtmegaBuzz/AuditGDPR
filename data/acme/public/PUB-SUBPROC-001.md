This page, maintained by Acme Corp Ltd (Dublin, Ireland), sets out the sub-processors that Acme engages when providing the Acme Beacon customer-engagement and analytics platform, and when handling its own customers' account data. Every vendor and sub-processor that handles personal data is engaged only under a written data processing agreement containing the terms required by GDPR Article 28(3), including processing only on Acme's documented instructions and the security measures required by Article 32.

Acme's current sub-processor categories include cloud hosting on AWS infrastructure in the eu-west-1 region (Ireland) and payment processing by Stripe. EU customer data in the Acme Beacon platform is hosted on AWS in eu-west-1 and is not stored in the United States for primary processing. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, Acme relies only on an adequacy decision under GDPR Article 45 (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for US recipients, such as Acme Corp Inc., Austin, Texas, for group administration and product support purposes) or on appropriate safeguards under Article 46(2)(c) standard contractual clauses. Following the CJEU Schrems II judgment, Acme carries out transfer impact assessments for transfers relying on standard contractual clauses and adds supplementary measures where required.

Acme notifies customers at least 30 days before any new sub-processor is engaged or an existing one is replaced, giving customers the opportunity to object, in line with the general written authorisation in Article 28(2). Under Acme's Data Processing Agreement, Acme remains fully liable to its customers for the performance of its sub-processors, in accordance with Article 28(4), and sub-processors are bound by obligations no less protective than those Acme owes directly. Customers have audit rights under Article 28(3)(h), satisfied primarily by provision of independent audit reports such as ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certificates, with on-site audits available where reports are insufficient. A current list of sub-processors and the countries where they process personal data is also available on request at privacy@acme.example.

## 1. About this sub-processor list

This notice, published by Acme Corp Ltd (Dublin, Ireland), sets out the categories of sub-processors Acme uses to deliver the Acme Beacon customer-engagement and analytics platform, consistent with the categories disclosed in Acme's privacy notice (updated 15 March 2026), which include cloud hosting on AWS in the eu-west-1 region (Ireland) and payment processing by Stripe. Acme acts as a controller for its own account, employee, website-visitor and marketing data, and as a processor for its customers' end-user data stored in Beacon. Acme maintains records of processing activities under GDPR Article 30, and a summary of processing purposes is published in the privacy notice.

The full sub-processor register, including the countries where each sub-processor processes personal data, is available on request at privacy@acme.example. Customers are notified of sub-processor changes in advance. Acme's Data Processing Agreement and audit information are published in the Acme Trust Center.

## 3. How Acme engages sub-processors

Every sub-processor listed in section 2 is engaged only under a written data processing agreement containing the terms required by GDPR Article 28(3). Under these agreements, each sub-processor:

- processes personal data only on Acme's documented instructions;
- ensures the confidentiality of its personnel;
- implements the security measures required by Article 32;
- assists Acme in responding to data-subject rights requests;
- deletes or returns all personal data at the end of the service.

Sub-processors are bound by obligations no less protective than those Acme owes its customers directly, and, in line with Article 28(4), Acme remains fully liable to its customers for the performance of its sub-processors.

Acme notifies customers at least 30 days before any new sub-processor is engaged or an existing one is replaced, giving customers the opportunity to object, consistent with the general written authorisation in Acme's Data Processing Agreement (Art. 28(2)). Questions or objections: privacy@acme.example.

## 4. Notification of changes and your right to object

Acme notifies customers at least 30 days before any new sub-processor is engaged or an existing one is replaced, giving customers the chance to object, consistent with the general written authorisation in Acme's Data Processing Agreement under GDPR Article 28(2).

To object to a proposed change, contact privacy@acme.example before the end of the 30-day notification period, including your customer account details and the sub-processor concerned. Acme will review every objection and respond before the change takes effect. Where an objection cannot be resolved, Acme will discuss available options with you, which may include discontinuing the affected service.

A current list of sub-processors and the countries where they process personal data is available on request at privacy@acme.example, and customers are notified of changes to sub-processors in advance.

## 5. International transfers

As stated in Acme's privacy notice (version dated 15 March 2026), personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area only where an adequacy decision applies under GDPR Article 45 — including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for recipients in the United States — or where appropriate safeguards are in place under Article 46(2)(c) standard contractual clauses.

EU customer data in the Acme Beacon platform is hosted on AWS infrastructure in the eu-west-1 region (Ireland) and is not stored in the United States for primary processing, as confirmed in Acme's Trust Center (updated 2 June 2026).

Acme's US parent company, Acme Corp Inc. (Austin, Texas), may access limited personal data for group administration and product support purposes; those transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Following the CJEU Schrems II judgment, Acme carries out transfer impact assessments for transfers relying on standard contractual clauses and adds supplementary measures where required.

Questions about international transfers: privacy@acme.example.

## 6. Audits and accountability

Acme's standard Data Processing Agreement, published in the Acme Trust Center, grants customers audit rights under GDPR Article 28(3)(h). These rights are satisfied primarily by the provision of independent audit reports, such as ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certificates, which are available through the Acme Trust Center. Where such reports are insufficient, on-site audits are available to customers.

Acme maintains records of processing activities under GDPR Article 30(1) as a controller and under Article 30(2) as a processor for the Acme Beacon platform. A summary of Acme's processing purposes is published in the privacy notice (updated 15 March 2026).

For audit requests, copies of reports, or questions about this sub-processor list, contact Acme's Legal & Privacy team at privacy@acme.example. Acme's Data Protection Officer, based in Dublin, Ireland, oversees this sub-processor list and Acme's sub-processor compliance programme.
