This Cookie Policy explains how Acme Corp Ltd ("Acme", "we") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, and how you can control them. It applies to visitors of Acme's website. For questions about this policy or your personal data, contact us at privacy@acme.example.

## 1. Introduction and scope

Acme Corp Ltd ("Acme", "we") is a provider of a B2B customer-engagement and analytics platform, with its EU establishment in Dublin, Ireland. This Cookie Policy explains the cookies and similar technologies that we set on our website, why we set them, and the choices available to you.

Acme's website sets strictly necessary cookies without your consent, because they are required for the site to function. Non-essential analytics and marketing cookies are set only after you have given your consent through our cookie banner.

This Cookie Policy forms part of the information we provide about how we handle your personal data, and should be read alongside our privacy notice, which explains our processing more broadly and how to exercise your data protection rights.

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, contact us at privacy@acme.example.

## 2. What cookies and similar technologies we use

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences, and to understand how the site is used. Alongside cookies, we may use similar technologies such as local storage, which work in comparable ways. We group the technologies we use into the categories below.

**Strictly necessary cookies.** These cookies are essential for our website to function – for example, to keep the site secure, to remember your cookie-banner choices, and to enable core features. Strictly necessary cookies are set without your consent, because they are required to provide the website you have requested.

**Analytics cookies.** These non-essential cookies collect pseudonymised information about how visitors use our website, such as pages visited and interactions, so we can measure and improve its performance. Analytics cookies are set only after you have given your consent through our cookie banner. Our processing of pseudonymised product-usage analytics for our own website also rests on our legitimate interests under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; you can object at any time using the opt-out link in our privacy notice.

**Marketing cookies.** These non-essential cookies help us measure and improve our direct-marketing emails, which we send only on the basis of your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Marketing cookies are likewise set only after you have given your consent through our cookie banner.

**Service and transactional emails.** Emails about your Acme Beacon account – such as security notices and billing messages – are sent on the basis of contract performance under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and are not marketing.

## 3. Legal bases for cookies and related processing

Under the GDPR, we may only process your personal data if we have a lawful basis under Art. 6. The table below maps each category of cookies and related processing described in this Cookie Policy to the lawful basis we rely on.

| Processing | Lawful basis | Your choice |
|---|---|---|
| Strictly necessary cookies | Required to provide the website you have requested | None – these are essential |
| Analytics cookies and pseudonymised product-usage analytics | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) | Object at any time via the opt-out link in our privacy notice |
| Marketing cookies | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) | Withdraw consent at any time via the marketing preferences page |
| Direct-marketing emails | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) | Withdraw consent at any time via the marketing preferences page |
| Service and transactional emails about your Acme Beacon account | Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) | Not marketing – no consent to withdraw |

**Consent-based processing.** We send direct-marketing emails only on the basis of your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and non-essential analytics and marketing cookies are set only after you have consented through our cookie banner. You may withdraw your consent at any time via the marketing preferences page, and withdrawal is as easy as giving consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR). We keep a record of when and how each consent was given, including the consent text version shown, so we can demonstrate compliance with Art. 5(2) GDPR.

**Legitimate interests.** We process pseudonymised product-usage analytics for our own website on the basis of our legitimate interests under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. You can object at any time using the opt-out link in our privacy notice.

**Contract performance.** Service and transactional emails about your Acme Beacon account are sent on the basis of contract performance under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. These are not marketing, so they are not affected by your marketing preferences.

## 4. Managing your cookie and marketing preferences

**Cookie banner choices.** When you first visit our website, our cookie banner lets you give or refuse consent to non-essential analytics and marketing cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are set without consent because they are required for the site to function. You can revisit your cookie-banner choices at any time through the cookie settings link on our website and change them whenever you wish.

**Marketing preferences page.** We send direct-marketing emails only on the basis of your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You may withdraw that consent at any time via the marketing preferences page. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it (Art. 7(3) GDPR): the same number of steps and the same ease of access apply in both directions. Once you withdraw consent, we will stop sending you direct-marketing emails and will not set marketing cookies on your device.

**Your right to object to direct marketing.** Independently of any consent decision, you have an absolute right to object to direct marketing at any time under Art. 21(2)-(3) GDPR. When you object, we stop that processing – there is no balancing test and no exceptions. You can object using the opt-out link in our marketing emails, the marketing preferences page, or by contacting us at privacy@acme.example.

**What withdrawal and objection do not affect.** Withdrawing consent or objecting does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before you withdrew. Service and transactional emails about your Acme Beacon account – such as security notices and billing messages – are sent on the basis of contract performance under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, are not marketing, and will continue.

**Our records.** We keep a record of when and how each consent was given, including the consent text version shown, so we can demonstrate compliance with Art. 5(2) GDPR. This includes records of withdrawals and objections.

## 5. How we demonstrate compliance

Under Art. 5(2) GDPR, we must be able to demonstrate that our consent-based processing complies with the GDPR. To do this, we keep a record of when and how each consent was given, including the consent text version shown to you at the time. These records cover consents given through our cookie banner for non-essential analytics and marketing cookies, and consents to receive our direct-marketing emails.

Our records also capture withdrawals of consent and objections to direct marketing, so we can show that we stopped the relevant processing when you asked us to. We use these records to respond to questions from you, from our lead supervisory authority, and for internal compliance reviews. If you would like more information about the consents we hold for you, contact us at privacy@acme.example.

## 6. Contact us and your rights

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, about the cookies we set, or about how we handle your personal data, contact us at privacy@acme.example.

You have rights in relation to your personal data, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Acme Corp Ltd's lead supervisory authority is the Irish Data Protection Commission. You also have an absolute right to object to direct marketing at any time under Art. 21(2)-(3) GDPR; when you object, we stop that processing. Our privacy notice explains your other rights and how to exercise them.
