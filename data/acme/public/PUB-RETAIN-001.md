This summary explains how long Acme Corp keeps personal data and what happens to it when it is no longer needed. It is published for our customers as part of our commitment to transparency under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), in particular the storage limitation principle in Article 5(1)(e), which requires personal data to be kept no longer than necessary for the purposes for which it is processed.

If you have questions about anything in this summary, contact us at privacy@acme.example.

## 1. About this summary

This document is Acme Corp's public summary of how long we keep personal data. It supplements our privacy notice, which explains who we are, what data we collect, why we process it, and your rights. This summary focuses on one question: when is your data deleted?

Under the GDPR's storage limitation principle (Article 5(1)(e)), personal data must be kept in a form which permits identification of data subjects for no longer than is necessary for the purposes for which it is processed. In practice, this means we do not keep your data indefinitely — each category of data has a defined retention period, and once that period ends, the data is deleted unless we are legally required to keep it longer.

If anything here differs from the privacy notice, the privacy notice prevails.

## 2. Customer account data

When you close your Acme Corp account, we delete the customer account data associated with it within 30 days of account closure. This is set out in our privacy notice and reflects the GDPR's storage limitation principle in Article 5(1)(e), which requires personal data to be kept no longer than necessary for the purposes for which it is processed.

You do not need to wait until account closure to act on your data. Our platform, Acme Beacon, includes a self-service page — Beacon Settings > Privacy — where you can export your account data or delete it yourself at any time.

If you would prefer to make a request through us instead, you can contact privacy@acme.example and we will handle it without undue delay, as required by Article 17(1) GDPR.

## 3. Billing and invoicing records

We keep billing and invoicing records for 6 years after the end of the financial year to which they relate. This retention period exists because Irish tax law requires us to retain these records for that length of time. Keeping them for this period is an exception to prompt deletion that is permitted under the GDPR: where a legal obligation requires retention, data may be kept for as long as that obligation applies.

Once the 6-year period ends, the billing and invoicing records are deleted. We do not keep them beyond that point.

If you have questions about how long a particular record is kept, contact us at privacy@acme.example.

## 4. Backups

Acme Corp keeps backups of its systems for 35 days, as stated on our trust center page. Backups exist to protect against data loss and to support recovery of our services, not to extend the life of data you have asked us to delete.

This means that data deleted at a customer's request is not restored from backup. If you delete your account data — through Beacon Settings > Privacy or via a request to privacy@acme.example — it stays deleted, and we do not bring it back from a backup during the 35-day backup retention window.

Questions about backups can be sent to privacy@acme.example.

## 5. Exceptions: legal holds and legal obligations

In some situations, we are legally required to keep personal data for longer than the periods described above. For example, if a legal hold applies, or if we need data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims — an exception recognised by Article 17(3)(e) GDPR — we may retain it beyond its normal retention period.

When we do this, we document the reason. The record explains which legal obligation or claim applies, which data it covers, and how long the retention is expected to last. Once the obligation no longer applies, the data is deleted.

If you have questions about whether an exception applies to your data, contact us at privacy@acme.example.

## 6. Requesting erasure of your personal data

You have the right to have your personal data erased. As our privacy notice explains, there are two ways to make an erasure request:

1. **Self-service:** Log in to Acme Beacon and go to Beacon Settings > Privacy, where you can delete your account data yourself.
2. **Contact us:** Email privacy@acme.example, and our team will handle your request for you.

Whichever route you choose, erasure requests are handled without undue delay, as required by Article 17(1) GDPR. We will confirm what has been deleted once your request is complete.

If you have questions about erasure, or you are unsure whether your request is covered, contact us at privacy@acme.example and we will help you through the process.

## 7. Questions and contact

If you have questions about the retention periods in this summary, or about your rights over your personal data, our privacy team is here to help. Email us at privacy@acme.example and we will respond as quickly as we can.

You can also exercise your rights directly through Acme Beacon: go to Beacon Settings > Privacy to export or delete your account data at any time. Erasure requests made either way are handled without undue delay, as required by Article 17(1) GDPR.
